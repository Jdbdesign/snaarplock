## 8. Security Architecture

**Eyebrow:** Security Architecture

**Headline:**
> We can't read your passwords.
> That's by design.

**Body (paragraph 1):**
> Zero-knowledge means your master password never leaves your device. We store only an encrypted blob that only you can decrypt. Even a court order couldn't make us hand over your data — because we simply don't have it.

**Body (paragraph 2):**
> Everything is encrypted locally using PBKDF2 key derivation and AES-256-GCM before it ever touches our servers.

**Security Badges (5 items):**

**Badge 1:**
- Title: `AES-256-GCM Encryption`
- Body: Military-grade cipher on every item in your vault, enforced locally on your device.

**Badge 2:**
- Title: `Zero-knowledge Architecture`
- Body: Your master password is never transmitted, stored, or seen — by anyone, including us.

**Badge 3:**
- Title: `PBKDF2 Key Derivation`
- Body: Your master password is stretched into an encryption key that resists brute-force attacks.

**Badge 4:**
- Title: `Independent Security Audits`
- Body: Penetration tests and code audits by third-party security firms — results published publicly.

**Badge 5 (wide/full-width):**
- Title: `24/7 Dark Web Monitoring`
- Body: We continuously scan billions of leaked credentials and alert you instantly if your email or username surfaces in any known data breach.

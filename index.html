Security Architecture

Built to protect you at every layer

Snaarplock's security model is engineered from the ground up — multiple independent layers keep your vault safe even if one layer fails.

End-to-End Encryption

Your vault is encrypted with AES-256 before it ever leaves your device. Only you hold the key — we never see your data, period.

Learn More

Zero-Knowledge Architecture

Snaarplock cannot read, access, or share your passwords — ever. Your master password is hashed locally and never transmitted to our servers.

Learn More

Two-Factor Authentication

TOTP apps, hardware security keys, and passkeys are all supported. A stolen master password still can't open your vault without the second factor.

Learn More

Breach Monitoring

We continuously scan billions of leaked credentials and alert you the moment any of your accounts appear in a known data breach.

Biometric Unlock

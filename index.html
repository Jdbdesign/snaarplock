Security Architecture

Built to protect you at every layer

Snaarplock's security model is engineered from the ground up — multiple independent layers keep your vault safe even if one layer fails.

1 · End-to-End Encryption

Your vault is encrypted with AES-256 before it ever leaves your device. Only you hold the key — we never see your data, period.

Live Session
2 · Zero-Knowledge Architecture

Snaarplock cannot read, access, or share your passwords — ever. Your master password is hashed locally and never transmitted.

Live Session
3 · Two-Factor Authentication

TOTP apps, hardware keys, and passkeys all supported. A stolen master password still can't open your vault without the second factor.

Live Session
4 · Breach Monitoring

We continuously scan billions of leaked credentials and alert you the moment any of your accounts appear in a known data breach.

Live Session
5 · Biometric Unlock

Unlock with Face ID, Touch ID, or Windows Hello — instant access on every trusted device without typing your master password again.
